Volkswagen Quantum Shuttle

🚦

The problem

At Lisbon's Web Summit in 2019, thousands of visitors crowded the roads, and buses could jam the very streets they were meant to help.

⚛️

The solution

Volkswagen used a D-Wave quantum annealer to plan a route for each of nine buses in near real time, so they spread out instead of piling onto one road.

Can quantum computers break encryption?

🔓

The problem

RSA protects much of today's online banking and messaging, and it is safe only because factoring a huge number is extremely slow for ordinary computers.

⚛️

The solution

Quantum technology changes the game on both sides: Shor's algorithm lets a large quantum computer factor huge numbers quickly, and quantum security tools like quantum key distribution build locks protected by physics.

🕵️

Harvest now, decrypt later

Attackers can record encrypted data today and unlock it years later, once a capable quantum computer exists.

🚀

Why quantum matters

Today's machines are still too small to break real keys, but quantum hardware improves every year. That is why industry and governments are investing in quantum technology and quantum-safe security now.
